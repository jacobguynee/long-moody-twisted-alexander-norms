Papers Manifest
===============

bigelow_tian_2008.pdf
  Title:   Generalized Long-Moody Representations of Braid Groups
  Authors: Stephen Bigelow, Jianjun Paul Tian
  Year:    2008
  Journal: Communications in Contemporary Mathematics, Vol. 10, Suppl. 1, pp. 1093-1102
  Source:  User's Downloads folder ("lm bigelow tian.pdf")
  Summary: Gives the algebraic (matrix-level) Long-Moody construction: input a
           representation of F_n ⋊ B_n, output a representation of B_n. Includes
           the universal construction (Section 2), subgroup version (Section 3),
           Gassner representation (Section 4), Lawrence/Hecke construction
           (Section 5), and the reduced Long-Moody construction (Section 6).
           Our code implements the reduced construction from Section 6.

long_1994.pdf
  Title:   Constructing Representations of Braid Groups
  Authors: D. D. Long
  Year:    1994
  Journal: Communications in Analysis and Geometry, Vol. 2, pp. 217-238
  Source:  https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~long/pubpdf/ConstructiongRepresentations1994.pdf
  Summary: Original Long-Moody construction. Shows how to build new braid group
           representations from existing ones by "adding a strand" using the
           Birman exact sequence. Proves the construction preserves faithfulness
           and irreducibility under certain conditions.

lawrence_1990.pdf
  Title:   Homological Representations of the Hecke Algebra
  Authors: R. J. Lawrence
  Year:    1990
  Journal: Communications in Mathematical Physics, Vol. 135, pp. 141-191
  Source:  https://projecteuclid.org/journals/communications-in-mathematical-physics/volume-135/issue-1/Homological-representations-of-the-Hecke-algebra/cmp/1104201923.pdf
  Summary: Topological construction of Hecke algebra representations via
           homology of configuration spaces with twisted local coefficient
           systems. The fibre is Y_{w,m} (configuration of m points in C
           minus n punctures), with two parameters alpha, q. When alpha = q^{-2},
           the representation factors through the Hecke algebra H_n(q) and
           gives the representation associated to the Young diagram Lambda_m
           (two rows, second row of length m). Sections 3-4 give explicit
           recursive formulas for the braid group action (Theorem 3.4) and
           the m=1 (Burau) and m=2 (Lawrence-Krammer) cases.
